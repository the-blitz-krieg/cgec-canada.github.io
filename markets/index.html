Why the EEA for Canadian Business

CETA eliminates tariffs on 98% of product categories and opens European government procurement to Canadian suppliers. EICBI's EU Parliament accreditation provides the institutional layer no trade agreement can give.

  • ✓ CETA provides 98% tariff-free access
  • ✓ EICBI's European Parliament accreditation
  • ✓ Direct access to EU institutional decision-makers
  • ✓ 27+ country market through one entry strategy
