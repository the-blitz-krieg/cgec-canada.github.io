FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does CGEC focus on UK, EEA and India?

CGEC focuses exclusively on UK, EEA, and India because these are the three markets where EICBI's 13 years of relationship-building gives Canadian businesses a genuine institutional advantage. CETA provides preferential access to the EEA, Commonwealth ties connect Canada to the UK and India, and EICBI's network spans all three corridors.

What is CETA and how does it help Canadian exporters?

CETA (Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement) eliminates tariffs on 98% of product categories between Canada and the EU, opens European government procurement markets to Canadian suppliers, and enables mutual recognition of professional qualifications.
