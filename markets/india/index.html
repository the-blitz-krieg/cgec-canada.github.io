Market Overview

India: A Generational Opportunity for Canadian Business

India has emerged as one of the most significant economic stories of the 21st century. With a $3.7 trillion GDP, a rapidly expanding middle class of over 400 million people, and one of the world's fastest-growing major economies, India represents a generational commercial opportunity for Canadian businesses.

Canada's relationship with India is uniquely positioned: a large and influential Indian diaspora community, Commonwealth ties, and bilateral diplomatic relationships create a foundation that few other countries can match.

What CGEC adds is decisive: EICBI's 13-year, ground-level network of relationships with Indian government bodies, business chambers, regional governments, and institutional leaders across India's major cities.

The EICBI Advantage

Why EICBI's India Network is Irreplaceable

Sector Opportunities

Priority Sectors for Canadian Business in India

Six sectors where CGEC identifies the strongest alignment between Canadian capability and Indian market demand.

Technology & AI

India's digital transformation creates extraordinary demand for Canadian technology expertise in AI, cybersecurity, and enterprise software.

Clean Energy

India's commitment to 500GW of renewable energy by 2030 creates significant demand for Canadian cleantech in solar, wind, green hydrogen, and grid management.

Agri-food & Food Security

Canada's agri-food technology, precision agriculture, and food safety expertise align well with India's food security priorities and processing sector.

Life Sciences & Health

India's generic pharmaceutical industry and rapidly growing private healthcare sector create partnership opportunities for Canadian life sciences companies.

Education & EdTech

India produces the world's largest number of STEM graduates annually. Canadian universities and EdTech companies find strong partnership demand.

Mining & Critical Minerals

Canada and India's complementary mineral resources and processing capabilities create strong bilateral partnership opportunities in critical minerals.
