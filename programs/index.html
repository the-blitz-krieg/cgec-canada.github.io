FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What trade missions does CGEC run?

CGEC runs structured trade missions to London UK, Brussels Belgium (European Parliament), and key Indian cities including Mumbai and New Delhi. Each mission includes pre-arranged B2B meetings, government introductions through EICBI's network, and sector-specific roundtables. The inaugural Canada-UK mission is scheduled for Spring 2026.

What are CGEC membership tiers?

CGEC offers three membership tiers: Associate Member (market intelligence, events, discounted missions), Full Member (adds market entry assessment, matchmaking sessions, priority mission allocation), and Strategic Member (adds multi-market assessments, government relations, EICBI parliamentary introductions, EuropeIndia40 nomination eligibility).
