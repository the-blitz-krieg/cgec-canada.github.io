About CGEC

Canada's dedicated export council for the UK, EU and Asia

We exist for one reason: to give Canadian businesses the institutional relationships, market intelligence and on-the-ground introductions that convert trade agreements into signed commercial relationships.

Our mission

Trade agreements open doors. CGEC walks you through them.

Canada's trade agreements with the United Kingdom and the European Union are among the most comprehensive in the world. CETA alone covers 98 percent of Canadian exports tariff-free into 450 million EU consumers. Yet most Canadian companies never fully exploit these agreements because market entry requires more than a favourable tariff schedule.

CGEC was founded to close that gap: to provide Canadian businesses with direct introductions to buyers, partners and institutional stakeholders across the three corridors where Canadian exports have the strongest competitive position.

We are a not-for-profit council, incorporated in Nova Scotia, with a membership model designed to ensure our interests are permanently aligned with those of the Canadian businesses we serve.

What sets us apart

The CGEC difference

Most export advisory services provide research reports. CGEC provides relationships.

Parliamentary access

EICBI partnership

As the exclusive Canadian partner of the Europe India Centre for Business and Industry, CGEC members gain access to a geopolitical forum that has been convening parliamentary summits in Brussels and London for more than 13 years. The EU Transparency Register confirmation number is 423661050520-44.

Verified introductions

Warm contacts, not cold lists

Every buyer, distributor and institutional partner introduction CGEC facilitates is pre-qualified and relationship-based. We do not deliver directories. We deliver meetings with decision-makers who are already briefed on your value proposition.

Sector expertise

Deep sector knowledge

CGEC focuses on sectors where Canadian companies have genuine competitive advantages: technology, cleantech, agri-food, professional services, and natural resources. Our advisors have hands-on experience in each corridor market.

Not-for-profit structure

Aligned incentives

CGEC is a not-for-profit council. Our membership fees fund our operations, and our mandate is the success of our members. We have no consulting fees to maximise, no billable hours to stretch, and no parent company with a competing advisory practice.

Multi-corridor strategy

Three corridors, one strategy

CGEC operates simultaneously across the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Asia. Our members benefit from intelligence and contacts across all three corridors from day one, even if they are entering only one market initially.

Nova Scotia base

Atlantic Canada gateway

Halifax is ideally positioned as Canada's closest major port to Europe and as a significant hub for trans-Atlantic trade. CGEC's Nova Scotia incorporation reflects our commitment to building export capacity across all of Canada, not just the major metropolitan centres.

Our structure

How CGEC is organised

Governance

CGEC is incorporated in Nova Scotia as a not-for-profit corporation under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act. The Council is governed by a board that includes sector advisors, market specialists and trade practitioners with direct experience in our three corridors.

Advisory structure

Our advisory panels bring together practitioners with direct market experience in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Asia. Advisors contribute to programme design, provide introductions from their own networks and review the quality of the intelligence we provide to members.

EICBI relationship

The Europe India Centre for Business and Industry is a European Commission registered geopolitical forum that has been accredited by the European Parliament since 2011. CGEC is EICBI's exclusive Canadian partner, giving CGEC members access to a parliamentary network that spans Brussels, London, New Delhi and Mumbai.

Membership model

CGEC operates on a membership model with three tiers: Associate, Full Member and Corporate Partner. Each tier provides a different level of programme access, introduction frequency and advisory support. All tiers receive access to CGEC market intelligence and event invitations.

Our values

What we stand for

Credibility over noise

CGEC will never claim access we do not have or introductions we cannot deliver. Our reputation in Brussels, London and Delhi depends on every introduction being worthwhile to both sides of the table.
