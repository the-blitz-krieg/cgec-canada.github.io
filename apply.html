What happens next
  1. You submit this form. We confirm receipt within one business day.
  2. Our team reviews your application (usually within three business days).
  3. We schedule a 30-minute introductory call to discuss your objectives and confirm the right membership tier.
  4. We send you a membership agreement and invoice.
  5. Once confirmed, your relationship manager is in touch within two business days to begin your onboarding.
Membership tiers at a glance
Associate

Market intelligence, events, annual corridor strategy session, one annual introduction.

Full Member

Market Entry Assessment, quarterly introductions, trade mission eligibility, regulatory advisory, dedicated relationship manager.

Corporate Partner

Dedicated market access manager, ongoing introductions, parliamentary summit participation, CGEC co-branding.
