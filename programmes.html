What CGEC delivers

From market intelligence to signed commercial relationships

CGEC runs four structured programmes. Each is designed to produce a concrete commercial outcome: a qualified introduction, a market-ready strategy, a trade mission relationship, or a regulatory pathway.

Programme 1

Market Entry Assessment

A rigorous, structured assessment of your export readiness and the right corridor for your product or service.

Most Canadian companies that have not exported before, or are entering a new market, underestimate the complexity of the target market and overestimate the transferability of their domestic value proposition. The CGEC Market Entry Assessment is designed to surface these gaps early, before they become expensive.

The assessment covers four areas: your product or service's competitive positioning in each of the three corridors; the specific regulatory and certification requirements you will need to satisfy; the realistic buyer and partner landscape; and the financial model for market entry including expected timelines to first revenue.

The output is a written Market Entry Report with a recommended corridor priority, a regulatory checklist and a shortlist of the specific buyers, distributors and institutional partners we recommend you engage first.

What you receive

  • Initial market corridor strategy session (90 minutes)
  • Written Market Entry Report (15 to 20 pages)
  • Regulatory requirements checklist by corridor
  • Shortlist of recommended first contacts
  • Review session to walk through findings

Who this is for

First-time exporters, companies entering a new corridor, and businesses that have been exporting informally and want a structured foundation for scaling their international operations.

Available to Full Members and Corporate Partners.

Programme 2

Parliamentary Trade Missions

Curated delegations to the European Parliament in Brussels, the UK Parliament in London and key centres in India.

CGEC trade missions are not tourist itineraries. Every bilateral meeting is pre-arranged and pre-briefed. Delegation members receive a meeting pack for each contact including a profile of the individual, their institutional context and the specific topics we have pre-negotiated as the agenda for your meeting.

EICBI's accreditation by the European Parliament gives CGEC missions access to parliamentary facilities, committee staff and MEPs in a way that no standard trade delegation can replicate. In London, EICBI's long-standing relationship with the UK Parliament provides equivalent access.

Missions are sector-focused, with a maximum of 12 Canadian companies per delegation to preserve the quality of each company's access and ensure the meetings are substantive rather than ceremonial.

Programme 3

Partner and Buyer Matching

Direct, verified introductions to buyers, distributors, institutional partners and procurement offices across all three corridors.

Partner and Buyer Matching is CGEC's core ongoing service for Full Members and Corporate Partners. On a quarterly basis, CGEC's market teams identify the best-fit contacts for each member company from across our active relationship networks in the UK, EEA and Asia, and facilitate direct introductions.

An introduction from CGEC is not a cold email. Before any introduction is made, CGEC briefs the receiving party on the Canadian company's value proposition, the specific opportunity we are proposing and the expected outcome of the initial conversation. Every introduction is warm, prepared and purposeful.

Corporate Partners receive a dedicated market access manager who proactively identifies and pursues introductions on an ongoing basis, rather than the quarterly matching cadence that applies to Full Members.

Introduction categories

  • Commercial buyers and procurement offices
  • Distribution and channel partners
  • Joint venture and licensing partners
  • Institutional investors and strategic investors
  • Government agencies and regulatory bodies
  • Research and academic institutions

Matching cadence

  • Associate Member: Annual introduction session
  • Full Member: Quarterly introductions, minimum 3 per quarter
  • Corporate Partner: Dedicated manager, ongoing introductions

Programme 4

Regulatory Advisory

Practical regulatory guidance and introductions to specialist advisors in the UK, EU and India.

Market access is not just a question of finding buyers. It is a question of meeting the regulatory requirements that allow you to sell to those buyers. The post-Brexit divergence of UK and EU regulations, the introduction of the EU AI Act, and the evolving certification landscape in India have all created new compliance requirements that Canadian exporters must understand before they can complete a transaction.

CGEC's Regulatory Advisory programme provides CGEC members with a practical orientation to the specific regulatory requirements they face in each corridor, and introductions to qualified specialist advisors who can take them through the compliance process.

We are not a law firm and we do not provide legal advice. What we provide is a map of the regulatory landscape, an honest assessment of what you need to do, and warm introductions to the advisors who can help you do it.
