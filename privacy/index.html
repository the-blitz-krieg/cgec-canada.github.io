Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

1. Who we are

Canada Global Export Council (CGEC) is a not-for-profit corporation incorporated in Nova Scotia, Canada. Our website is cgec.ca. References to "we," "us" and "our" in this policy refer to CGEC. You can contact us at info@cgec.ca.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you submit a membership application, send us a contact form message, subscribe to our newsletter or register for an event. This information may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, province and sector.

We also collect information automatically when you visit cgec.ca, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring URL. This is collected through analytics tools and is used in aggregated form to understand how our website is used.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiries and applications, send you CGEC programme updates and event invitations you have requested, administer your CGEC membership, improve our website and services and comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) or the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process your personal information on the basis of your consent (where you have provided it), the performance of a contract (for membership administration), our legitimate interests in operating CGEC and communicating with prospective members, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting or reporting requirements. Membership records are retained for seven years following the end of a membership. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@cgec.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and analytics

cgec.ca uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the website and to understand usage patterns. Analytics data is collected in aggregated form. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though this may affect website functionality.

8. Third-party services

cgec.ca uses Google Fonts (fonts served from Google's servers), Google Analytics or a privacy-respecting alternative (aggregated analytics), and a third-party form processing service for contact and application forms. Each of these services is subject to its own privacy policy.

9. International transfers

CGEC is based in Canada. If you are located outside Canada, your information may be transferred to and processed in Canada, which has privacy laws that may differ from those in your jurisdiction. For European visitors, we rely on the EU-Canada adequacy decision under GDPR.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests or complaints, please contact us at info@cgec.ca or write to Canada Global Export Council, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada.
