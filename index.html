Our corridors

Three markets. One council.

Every CGEC programme operates across all three corridors, so your market strategy is never limited to a single trade lane.

United Kingdom

Post-Brexit opportunity market

  • Canada-UK Trade Continuity Agreement in force
  • NHS: one of the world's largest single buyers
  • London fintech and FCA sandbox access
  • UK retailers diversifying away from EU suppliers
  • Parliamentary introductions via EICBI

European Economic Area

CETA-powered market access

  • CETA covers 98% of Canadian exports tariff-free
  • EU Green Deal and Critical Raw Materials Act
  • European Parliament network via EICBI
  • EU procurement and Horizon Europe R and D funding
  • 450 million consumers, single regulatory framework

Asia

EICBI's 13-year India network

  • India: fastest-growing major economy
  • 500 GW renewable energy target by 2030
  • World's largest STEM graduate pipeline
  • UPI fintech infrastructure and digital markets
  • Direct government introductions via EICBI
