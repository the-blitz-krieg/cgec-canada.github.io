What is EICBI

A parliamentary forum for EU-India-Canada relations

The Europe India Centre for Business and Industry is a geopolitical forum that was founded in 2011 to build institutional and commercial bridges between Europe and India. Since its founding, EICBI has expanded its mandate to include Canada, the United Kingdom and other strategic economies.

EICBI is registered on the European Commission's EU Transparency Register at number 423661050520-44 and has been accredited by the European Parliament. This accreditation is meaningful: it gives EICBI formal standing in Brussels that most trade associations, commercial chambers and advisory firms cannot access.

EICBI convenes parliamentary summits, bilateral ministerial meetings and senior business delegations in Brussels, Strasbourg, London, Westminster, New Delhi and Mumbai. It has relationships across the European Parliament's political groupings, the UK Parliament's select committees and India's parliamentary and government leadership.

What EICBI access means

Parliamentary access for Canadian businesses

EICBI accreditation provides a level of institutional access that no commercial advisory firm can replicate. Here is what it means in practice for CGEC members.

Brussels

European Parliament

EICBI accreditation gives CGEC access to the European Parliament's committee system, parliamentary events and MEP networks across political groupings. CGEC members can be included in formal European Parliament event delegations.

London

UK Parliament

EICBI's longstanding relationship with the UK Parliament provides access to select committees, All-Party Parliamentary Groups and cross-party parliamentary networks. CGEC trade missions to London can include Westminster access that standard commercial delegations cannot obtain.

New Delhi and Mumbai

Indian Government

EICBI has maintained relationships with Indian government ministries, Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) and Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI) since 2011. CGEC members benefit from introductions to Indian government and institutional buyers through a network built over 13 years.

European Commission

EU Policy

EICBI's registration on the EU Transparency Register means it participates formally in European Commission consultation processes. Canadian companies in sectors affected by EU regulation can benefit from EICBI's visibility into regulatory developments before they become law.

Parliamentary Summits

Summit participation

EICBI convenes bilateral parliamentary summits that bring together European, UK and Indian parliamentary figures. CGEC Corporate Partners can participate in these summits as part of the Canadian delegation, gaining access to a political and institutional network that is simply not available through normal commercial channels.

Exclusive Canadian partner

Canada's seat at the table

CGEC is EICBI's exclusive Canadian partner. This means no other Canadian trade association, chamber of commerce or advisory firm has the same institutional standing or access to the EICBI parliamentary network. CGEC members hold Canada's seat at the EICBI table.

Why it matters

Why parliamentary access translates to commercial outcomes

It is reasonable to ask why a Canadian exporter should care about the European Parliament or the Indian Rajya Sabha. The answer is that in regulated sectors, government relationships precede commercial ones.

A Canadian healthtech company seeking NHS procurement access needs a champion inside the NHS. A Canadian cleantech company competing for EU Green Deal procurement needs visibility in the relevant European Commission directorate-general. A Canadian technology company entering India's government digital infrastructure market needs relationships with the ministries that control those procurement decisions.

EICBI's parliamentary relationships are not ceremonial. They are the front door to the institutional buyers and government procurement offices that represent the largest, most durable commercial opportunities in each of our three corridors. That is why CGEC built its value proposition around this partnership rather than around conventional trade promotion.

Most export support gets you a list of companies you could email. EICBI gets you into the room where the decisions are made.
