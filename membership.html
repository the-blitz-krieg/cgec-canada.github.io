Membership

Three tiers. One council. Your market.

CGEC membership is designed for Canadian businesses at every stage of their export journey. Choose the tier that matches where you are today, and upgrade as your export programme matures.

Membership tiers

Associate Member

Associate

For export-curious businesses taking their first steps

  • Access to CGEC market intelligence and reports
  • Invitations to CGEC events and webinars
  • Annual corridor strategy session (60 minutes)
  • Annual introduction session (1 introduction)
  • Listing in the CGEC member directory
  • CGEC newsletter and trade policy updates
Apply as Associate
Most popular

Full Member

For businesses actively pursuing export growth

  • Everything in Associate
  • Market Entry Assessment (written report)
  • Quarterly introductions (minimum 3 per quarter)
  • Trade mission eligibility across all three corridors
  • Regulatory Advisory programme access
  • Priority EICBI parliamentary event invitations
  • Bilateral meetings at CGEC-hosted parliamentary events
  • Dedicated CGEC relationship manager
Apply as Full Member
Corporate Partner

Corporate Partner

For companies with a serious, sustained export mandate

  • Everything in Full Member
  • Dedicated market access manager
  • Ongoing introductions (no quarterly cadence cap)
  • Custom market entry strategy development
  • Parliamentary summit participation (Brussels and London)
  • CGEC co-branding on trade mission materials
  • Exclusive briefings with EICBI parliamentary contacts
  • Priority placement on all CGEC market delegations
  • Annual strategic review with CGEC leadership
Apply as Corporate Partner

Pricing is provided on application. All tiers are annual memberships. CGEC does not charge per-introduction or per-mission fees above membership.

Frequently asked questions

Membership questions

CGEC reviews every application to confirm that the applicant is a Canadian business with a genuine export objective, the product or service is appropriate for one or more of our three corridors, and the company has the capacity to follow through on the introductions and programme activities that membership provides. We do not accept applications from companies whose primary purpose is consulting or reselling CGEC introductions to third parties.

CGEC's primary focus sectors are technology, cleantech, agri-food and beverage, professional services, natural resources, healthcare technology and financial technology. We will consider applications from companies in other sectors on a case-by-case basis where there is a clear corridor fit and CGEC can deliver meaningful introductions.

The Market Entry Assessment programme, available to Full Members, is specifically designed for companies at this stage.

Yes. Members can upgrade their tier at any time during their membership year. The fee difference is prorated. Many members start as Associates, use the first year to assess corridor fit, and upgrade to Full Member when they are ready to begin active introductions.

No. CGEC does not charge fees above the annual membership for introductions or trade mission participation within your tier's entitlement. Travel and accommodation for trade missions are the member's own responsibility. There are no per-introduction success fees.

CGEC membership is available to Canadian businesses and organisations. International companies with a significant Canadian subsidiary, a Canada-based procurement office, or a material Canadian component to their operations may be considered. Please contact us to discuss your specific situation before applying.
